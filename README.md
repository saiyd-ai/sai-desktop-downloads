# Sai for Mac: downloads

Installers for **Sai**, the Saiyd.ai helper, as a Mac desktop app. This repository holds only the installer files
(on the Releases page). Sai needs a Saiyd.ai staff login to work.

Staff: get Sai from Saiyd.ai itself. Click Sai and choose **Get Sai on your desktop**.

- **Apple chip** Macs (M1 and newer): `Sai-<version>-arm64.dmg`
- **Intel** Macs: `Sai-<version>-x64.dmg`

Install: open the file, drag Sai into Applications, then open Sai. If your Mac says it can't check Sai, go to
System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**. Then sign in once with your Saiyd.ai login.
